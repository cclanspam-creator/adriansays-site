### The Nature of Rest

I derive rest from sleep, reverie, and solitude. Some people include other things, such as having a good meal, exercising, engaging in a hobby, reading, watching a movie, playing games, shopping, to name a few.

Clearly, how one rests is a personal thing. Nonetheless, I believe there are three levels of rest to be achieved.

#### Level 1: Physiological

To rest at this level is to reduce one’s activity and output. Our energy levels climb down from its peak. We metabolize less, and allow our body to process biochemical waste at the cellular level and refresh itself.

For me, sleep performs this function beautifully. Without good sleep, I wake up even more tired. With disrupted energy for the day, I lose my cycle of sleep, which results in more interrupted sleep.

These days, I have not been sleeping well due to seasonal allergies. As a result, my normal wake-up time in the wee morning hours have been chipped away gradually. Some days, I wake up at 9am, which is a far cry from my usual 4.30am.
