What is an Authentic Communal Exploration (ACE)? What does this research approach look like? The ACE Research Approach is something I put together in order to utilise research for a different purpose.

Unlike typical research which jumps into a tunnel focus on an issue or a problem, in ACE, at least 60% of the time is dedicated to self and community before attending to the issue at hand.

## Authentic Connections

The first step is generating authentic connections (how do I as a researcher relate to the issue at hand). It is about what I can offer to the issue at hand given what I have experienced.

## Communal Connections

The next step is to create communal connections (I am part of a research community whose dynamic influence on each other will influence our research, which compels me to call out these dynamics). For any research endeavour to work, a minimal amount of relationship is required. For research endeavour to flourish, the optimal relationship within the team needs to be established. Rules, roles, expectations, backing-up behaviours, specialisations, teamwork....doing research together can break existing relationships or strengthen them.

## Exploratory Connections

The final step is to get into the research proper

Typical Research Approach versus the ACE Research Approach

|                                             |                                                                                                                                                       |
|---------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| What is it? | Authentic Communal Exploration                                                                                                                        |
| What is the focus? | Mix of self, the research team, and the research constructs of interest                                                                               |
| Is it Fundamental or Applied                | It is a Fundamental (or primary) research approach aimed at discovering and understanding                                                             |
| Is it Quantitative, Qualitative or mixed? | It is typically mixed methods in design                                                                                                               |
| Is it exploratory or confirmatory           | It typically starts out more exploratory but can evolve to a confirmatory approach                                                                    |
| Is it longitudinal or cross-sectional       | It is typically cross-sectional, but can be either, depending on the time allocated                                                                   |
| Is it field-based or lab-based? | It is field-based                                                                                                                                     |
| Is it fixed or flexible in design? | It is flexible - it can involve case studies, ethnographic studies or grounded theory approaches                                                      |
| Is it action research or policy research? | It can be either                                                                                                                                      |
| Is it for classification or for comparison? | It can be either                                                                                                                                      |
| Is it causal, inductive or deductive? | It is inductive - the idea is to build theories and deepen understanding of an issue. The possibility of confirming hypotheses only comes about later |
