In my previous [article](https://adriansays.com/index.php/2020/04/26/facter-for-good/), I wrote at a macro level about how we can be a FACTER community to enable consistent good. Since then, I've been asked the next question regarding the "how to's", strategies and execution details. In this article, instead of focusing on big picture issues, I'd like to address something simple and universal, which all of us can do. It is simply that we become better at being friends and making friends.

## Be a FACTER, Be a Friend

If we are to become a FACTER community, then we must first be friends. There is much written about the art of making friends. For example, "[How to Win Friends and Influence People](https://www.amazon.com/How-Win-Friends-Influence-People/dp/0671027034)" by Dale Carnegie was an instant classic when it was first published in 1936 and still remains one of the most enduring best seller even now. I don't feel I have much else to add to the matter.

To be a FACTER for good, let's count our friendships instead.

- Funder: Do we have a friend who is a funder, who believes in you enough to put good money on you?
- Activator: Do you have friends who are activated for good, who believes in and are invested in doing good?
- Collaborator: Are you able to collaborate with your friends in the good works of others, and celebrate being able to do so?
- Tribe: Is there a particular tribe that you care for enough, are friends with, that you want to do what you can for them?
- Ecclesia (Activated citizenry): Are you a person whom others look up to as an influence for good?
- Research: And are you able to enact or call upon friends who will help you do the necessary research for doing good in places that matter?

As you can see, we are not just talking about personal networks, but **purposeful** networks that we can activate for good.

## Expression of Purpose

If you found yourself deficient in purposeful friendships, it's alright. Most people make friends because of mutual likeability. These are friendships made for pleasure or mutual pursuits. And most of the time, we are happy to let friendships remain at that amicable level.

To be a FACTER friend, a transition needs to occur. Those whom we've known as friends of old, we now need to redefine those friendships to become friends for a greater purpose. And not all friendships can weather this transformation. Clearly, there is a way to help our valued friends become friends of purpose.

### Our Inherent Need and Gift

You may ask, "What's so difficult about making friends?" Well, normally, it should not be difficult. Just take a walk to any playground with toddlers and young children, and you will find friendships and co-operative play that strike up naturally amongst them. On the contrary, how NOT to befriend is a learned behaviors - just take a look at the attendant parents and care-givers at the playground. They are the ones who restrain friendships from occurring. They are the ones who judge "That boy is not of the same race" or "That girl looks so dirty" or "How naughty these kids are - better stay away from them."
