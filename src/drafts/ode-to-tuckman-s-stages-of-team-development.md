> > > Tuckman's model from an identity perspective. Tuckman calls it stages. I submit that it's a journey with many twists and turns, occasionally advancing, often retracing, but always, a co-journeying together. 
> > >
> > > **Form**: I am amongst you. I have just arrived. I haven't unpacked, and my heart’s still in transit. My presence may be disruptive to you. May we be kind to each other.
> > >
> > > **Storm**: I don't know when it began, but it is here. We are storm bringers to each other. We ignite in each other things we are not proud of. It's a tussle for space, a mourning of loss, a yearning to return to what it was. This visible storm begins with the secret storm within. I may be here, but I am still full of me. There is still too much of me to have any left for you. And I wonder if it's the same for you. When does this storm end? Is it when I can calm the storm within me? Or is it when my presence amidst the storms around us is of comfort to you? Or perhaps it starts to end when I finally see that I must be all in for you. For surely it ends when you see that I am actually, truly, fully, all for you. With you.
> > >
> > > **Norm**: "Norm" is such a strange word, an Anglo-Saxon name from an era past. It tastes like we are get hammered to a common height, beaten into shape, leached of all that is uniquely us. If that's "norm", then no, let's not do norm. Instead, let's be neighbours. For we are now in a same common space. Let's learn to see each other. Let's learn to get used to being neighbours together. Let's be part Samaritans, part servants, part suppliers of good food and cheer, part sojourners for the journeys we are on together. It's a long march, but it doesn't have to be done alone. So…hello Norm, or whatever your name is. Welcome to our space.
> > >
> > > **Perform**: Ah Tuckman, oh Tuckman. So industrial, so perfunctory! I am not a puppet on tethered strings. Nor am I a seal in a circus. I don’t do perform. But I will do great and wonderful things for and with my kin, because we are now more than neighbours. We are friends, family, kin and of the same kingdom. I will pour fully of myself to those I am called. And I know they will do likewise. Occasionally, we may “perform” mightily, visibly. But mostly, we love each other willingly, unceasingly. And together, being likewise, we inspire likewise to others until they are wise like us.
> > >
> > > Adrian Chan, 7 June 2024
