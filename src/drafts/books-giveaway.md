There truly is nothing new under the sun. Much of research uncovered is actually insight rediscovered. Hence, knowledge objectified in books are as impermanent as it gets.

Given this, I suppose the best compliment one can pay to books is to give the best ones away. So here are some books which I'd like to give away:

1. 40 Studies that Changed Psychology by Roger Hock
2. Handbook of Self and Identity by Leary & Tangney
3. The Nature of Leadership by Antonakis, Cianciolo & Sternberg
4. Handbook of Leadership by Bass, Stogdill
5. The Leadership Experience in Asia by Lim & Daft
6. Competent Communication by O'Hair, Friedrich, Wiemann & Wiemann
7. Using Tests to Improve Training by Reddin
8. How to Design and Evaluate Research in Education by Fraenkel & Wallen
9. Methods of Meta-Analysis by Hunter & Schmidt
10. Social Network Analysis by Wasserman & Faust
11. Redirect: The Surprising New Science of Psychological Change by Timothy Wilson
12. Connected: The Amazing Power of Social Networks and How They Shape Our Lives by Christakis and Fowler
13. Thinking Fast and Slow by Danny Kahneman
14. On Leadership by John Gardner
15. In Over Our Heads by Robert Kegan
16. Frames of Mind: The Theory of Multiple Intelligences by Howard Gardner
17. Capital in the 21st Century by Thomas Piketty

Please contact me [here](https://adriansays.com/index.php/contact-me/) with your mailing address and the book you would like to receive.

Suggestion: If you are done with a book, why don't you give it away?
