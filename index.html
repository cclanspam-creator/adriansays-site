Adrian Says

I am a psychologist by training and a note-taker by habit. I spent twenty-two years in the army and eight years helping the Kingdom of Bhutan grow its leaders, and I still spend most of my waking hours wondering why people do what they do (myself included). I believe leaders are made slowly, one honest reflection at a time. This blog is where I think out loud about leadership, faith and the small nudges that make us better, in the hope that some of it bears good fruit for you too. More about me
